Monitor, maintain and explore your Mac.

One native app for system monitoring, power management, cleanup, networking and the everyday tools you reach for.

Download for macOS View source

Free and open source. Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 27 or later.

Everything your Mac is doing, in one place.

Live numbers where you want them, and the tools to act on them.

Live system monitoring

CPU, memory, disk, network and sensors, with the hottest sensor's trend and the indicators you choose in the menu bar.

Connection diagnosis

One click measures modem, internet and DNS separately and tells you where the problem is. Per-app traffic and Wi-Fi analysis included.

Disk analysis and cleanup

Open the "System Data" bucket and see what takes the space, classified Safe, Review or Manual, with the exact command shown before anything is deleted.

Window switcher

Alt+Tab-style cards with live previews, independent filters per shortcut and customizable window actions.

SSH manager

Lossless ssh_config editing, connection tests, keys, known hosts and backups, with quick connect from a shortcut.

AI usage tracking

Session and weekly quotas for Claude, Codex and Grok accounts, with menu bar indicators and threshold alerts.

Power and fans

Battery health, power schedule and fan profiles that always hand control back to macOS when something looks wrong.

Everyday tools

Clipboard history, screenshots, case converter, JSON, JWT, hashes, QR codes and more. Offline and always at hand.

Local-first, by design.

MacPocket has no account and sends no analytics. Each permission is requested only when the feature that needs it is used.

  • Your clipboard, window titles and file listings never leave the Mac.
  • Updates are signed and delivered through GitHub Releases.
  • The privileged helper exposes a narrow, validated interface.
  • The whole source is public under the MIT License.
